# Ferrari F60 V8 engine recordings

Recording: “Ferrari F60 (2009)” by Edvvc / Ed Pond, Goodwood Festival of Speed,
3 July 2010. A real Ferrari Tipo 056 naturally aspirated Formula One V8.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferrari_F60_(2009).ogg
Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/aa/Ferrari_F60_%282009%29.ogg

ferrari-f60-v8.ogg is the original recording. v8-mid.wav and v8-high.wav are
adaptations: mono conversion, trimming, high-pass filtering, crossfaded seams
and level normalization. Both derived files remain CC BY-SA 3.0. They may be
copied or remixed under that licence, with attribution and an indication of changes.
No endorsement by the original author is implied.

Rebuild with tools/build-v8-audio.py. Engine playback pitch follows RPM with
crossfaded load layers. Reference RPM values are approximate pitch calibrations,
not measured engine telemetry. The chassis uses modern dimensions with this
historic V8 sound as an intentional game configuration.
